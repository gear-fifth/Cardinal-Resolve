Vendor Credentialing & Setup Guidelines Read Before Submitting

To link your community to existing vendors, no ticket is needed.

Reminder, always search for vendors by their Vendor EIN (Tax ID on W9) and confirm the remit address in Vendor Credentialing is correct before linking your community. If the Vendor EIN matches but the remit address doesn’t match, submit a ticket by selecting the category “Update Existing Vendor Information" and "Create Additional remit location".

How to Link Existing Vendor to Community

Complete this ticket to complete any of the following items:

  • Set Up a New Vendor (Onsite): Please inform the vendor of the “next steps” in the vendor enrollment process. You won't see the vendor in Ops Tech until they are approved, but you can search for them in Vendor Credentialing (VC).
  • Update Existing Vendor Information: Change Existing Remit Address or Change Primary Email on Vendor Credentialing Account.
  • Set Up an Existing Vendor as Exempt: Exempt Vendor Types: Attorneys, Auditors, Banks, Charitable Organizations, Consultants, Government Entities, Hotel, Conference Centers, Meeting Halls, Insurance Companies, Locators, Marketing/Ad Agencies, Mortgage Companies/Lenders, Newspapers, Schools, Utility Companies, Materials only – Supplier (No On-Site Delivery).
  • Set Up a Team Member for Reimbursement
  • Submit a Vendor Credentialing Client Waiver: This Service Request should only be used after the community has been linked to the vendor. The client waiver applies to insurance issues only. Failed background checks do not apply to the waiver. The vendor must still complete the vendor credentialing process. Do not submit this form unless you have checked all the boxes that apply (Required Amount, Amount Carried) and added the circumstances why the vendor won’t or shouldn’t become credentialed.
  • Submit Transition Vendor WorkBook: Transition Vendors are deactivated 60 days after the Community’s “Go Live” Date. All Vendors need to become approved in Vendor Credentialing by that deadline.
  • Submit a Vendor Question
  • Set Up a New Vendor (Corporate Accounting Only): This ticket is only used for vendors who do not go on-site.
