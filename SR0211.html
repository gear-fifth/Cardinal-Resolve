Entrata Disable Auto Advance & Auto Posting of GPR Adjustments for Historical Site Read Before Submitting

This Service Request is to be used to request that two settings are disabled for a historical community:

  • Auto Advancing of the Period
  • Auto Posting of GPR Adjustments
Standard:
  • SR must be entered by the position Accounting Analyst or above
  • SR can only be requested on month two of the community being dispositioned
