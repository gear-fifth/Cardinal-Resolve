Hardware Request — Policy & Approval Guidelines Read Before Submitting

All HQ and Community Hardware Requests require prior approval based on total cost and requester role before fulfillment. Please review the guidelines below prior to submitting your request:

New Hire Hardware Policy
  • Pre-Approval: Requests for New Hires are automatically pre-approved for standard hardware requirements.
  • Lead Time: This SR must be submitted at least 15 days prior to the New Hire's joining date.
  • Short-Notice / Start Date Changes: If the Team Member starts in less than 15 days or if the start date changes after submission, notify IT immediately (at least 2 days prior to the change/start date) so we can arrange a temporary loaner laptop.
Approval Rules: Requests Under $2,500
  • CM/Manager or higher: Assumed approved (must obtain approval prior to submitting this SR).
  • TM (below CM): Approval required as part of the SR workflow.
Approval Rules: Requests $2,500 or More
  • PM or higher: Assumed approved (must obtain approval prior to submitting this SR).
  • CM or lower: Approval required as part of the SR workflow.
MacBook Request Requirements
  • Valid Reason: Provide a valid business justification explaining why a MacBook is required.
  • Support & Risk Acknowledgment: confirming you understand IT will have trouble supporting Mac and our software does not fully support macOS, key Cardinal tools such as OfficeConnect, Check Scanning are incompatible with Mac operating systems, and you accept these risks.
Hardware Availability Disclaimer: Standard laptop models, configurations, and OEMs are subject to change based on availability at the time of processing.
